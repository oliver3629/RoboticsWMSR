# References

- **Robotarium software:** [Official Python simulator](https://github.com/robotarium/robotarium_python_simulator), used for simulation, kinematics, and safety barriers. MIT license retained in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- **Ordinary consensus:** R. Olfati-Saber, J. A. Fax, and R. M. Murray. *Consensus and Cooperation in Networked Multi-Agent Systems.* Proceedings of the IEEE, 95(1), 215-233, 2007. [Author page](https://www.cds.caltech.edu/~murray/papers/2007c_ofm07-ieeeproc.html).
- **W-MSR:** H. Zhang and S. Sundaram. *Robustness of Information Diffusion Algorithms to Locally Bounded Adversaries.* arXiv:1110.3843v2, 2012, Section III-B. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.3843v2). Applied separately to x/y with F=1 and a 0.25 relaxation factor.
- **Robotarium platform:** S. Wilson et al. *The Robotarium: Globally Impactful Opportunities, Challenges, and Lessons Learned in Remote-Access, Distributed Control of Multirobot Systems.* IEEE Control Systems Magazine, 40(1), 26-44, 2020. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2019.2949973).
- **Safety barrier background:** L. Wang, A. Ames, and M. Egerstedt. *Safety Barrier Certificates for Heterogeneous Multi-Robot Systems.* American Control Conference, 5213-5218, 2016. [Record](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/z56n3-9q369). The project calls Robotarium's implementation.
